# Veteran Opportunities

### Benefits, careers, education, and community for life during and after service.

A community resource directory for **U.S. veterans, transitioning service members, military spouses, and families**. Find a useful starting point, understand who a program serves, and go directly to the organization that runs it.

**Maintained by Tech with Victoria** · Initial source review: **September 6, 2026**

[Start here](#start-here) · [Browse resources](#browse-resources) · [Suggest a resource](https://github.com/techwithvictoria/veteran-opportunities/issues/new/choose) · [Report a correction](https://github.com/techwithvictoria/veteran-opportunities/issues/new/choose)

> **Need support now?** Contact the [Veterans Crisis Line](https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/): **dial 988, then press 1**, text **838255**, or use online chat. You do not need to be enrolled in VA benefits or health care. For homelessness or risk of homelessness, call **877-424-3838** or visit [VA Homeless Programs](https://department.va.gov/homeless/).

| :--- | :--- |
| Understand your benefits | Explore [VA benefits](https://www.va.gov/) and connect with an [accredited representative or VSO](https://www.va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative/). |

### How to read this directory

**Program** means a standing resource page; it does not mean applications are open. **Cohort / annual / local** means dates, seats, or location affect availability. Each linked name is the source for its row.

Audience descriptions are starting points, not eligibility decisions. Check the provider's current rules, costs, and application dates before applying. Unless labeled otherwise, resources are U.S.-focused; online access does not necessarily mean worldwide eligibility. This is an independent community directory, not a VA or government website.

## Contributing

Know a resource that belongs here? [Open an issue](https://github.com/techwithvictoria/veteran-opportunities/issues/new/choose) or submit a pull request. A link and a short explanation are enough to start.

Please include the official URL, what the resource offers, who qualifies, location, costs, and any deadline **with its year and time zone**. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the review process.

**Source review:** Linked provider pages were reviewed on September 6, 2026. This confirms the resource descriptions at that time, not individual eligibility or live application availability. Update review dates only after checking the source. This is a growing directory, not an exhaustive list of every federal, state, county, or nonprofit program.

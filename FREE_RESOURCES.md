# Free Online and Local Resources for Veterans

[Back to the directory](README.md) · [Veteran retreats](RETREATS.md)

**Source review: September 6, 2026.** Free means the specific service described below, subject to eligibility and capacity. A free introductory course does not make an entire school free. Local travel, personal equipment, internet access, and ongoing ownership costs may remain your responsibility.

## Online learning and career tools

| Resource and source | What is free | Who can use it / how to start | Limits and availability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Salesforce Military](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/vetforce/know-vetforce) | Self-paced technology training and access to eligible certification benefits. | Current and transitioning military, veterans, and spouses; join the military community and complete the required learning path. | Verification and voucher requirements apply. Use the current program's instructions before paying for an exam. |
| [LinkedIn Premium — military community](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a518653/) | One complimentary 12-month Premium subscription. | Eligible veteran, military/veteran spouse, or transitioning active-duty job seeker **actively participating in an authorized nonprofit partner's career services**. Partners listed include SVA, Hire Heroes USA, and Hiring Our Heroes. | **Not an automatic annual renewal.** One-time offer, no active Premium subscription at redemption, limited codes. Ends automatically after the promotion. |
| [Code Platoon — Intro to Python](https://launchpoint.codeplatoon.org/course/intro-to-coding) | Free self-paced beginner coding course. | Online learners exploring programming, including military-connected beginners. | This listing covers the introductory course only; immersive bootcamp tuition and scholarships are separate. |
| [PsychArmor](https://psycharmor.org/frequently-asked-questions) | Individual access to online military-culture and support-related learning. | Veterans, families, caregivers, and others supporting the military community. | Free individual e-learning; customized organizational training is a separate offering. |
| [PVA Veterans Career Program](https://pva.org/find-support/veterans-career-program/) | Employment support, vocational counseling, toolkits, and career resources. | **All veterans**, transitioning service members, spouses, and caregivers; paralysis or PVA membership is not the stated audience restriction. | Connect with the team; also use its linked virtual seminars and recordings. |
| [USO transition support](https://www.uso.org/programs/uso-support-for-transitioning-service-members) | Transition specialist support, professional development resources, and career planning. | Active-duty, Reserve, National Guard members, and military spouses. | Transition-focused eligibility; do not assume every long-separated veteran qualifies. Register through Connect with a USO Specialist. |
| [FourBlock Scout](https://www.fourblock.org/https/scout.fourblock.org/) | AI-assisted career-readiness platform. | Veterans, service members, and military spouses. | Use the provider's Scout link. This listing does not imply every FourBlock partner product or course is included. |

Also see [O2O](README.md#tech-training-and-certifications), [Hire Heroes USA and ACP](README.md#careers-and-transition), and [Service to School](README.md#education-and-scholarships) in the main directory.

## Arts, well-being, and community from home

| Resource and source | What is free | Audience / delivery | Limits and next step |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Armed Services Arts Partnership (ASAP)](https://asapasap.org/) | Art, writing, storytelling, comedy, improv, and other creative classes. | Veterans, service members, military spouses, families, caregivers, and survivors. Online and selected local chapters. | Classes and workshops can have applications, deadlines, and waitlists. Use Explore Classes or the Online chapter. |
| [Veterans Yoga Project](https://veteransyogaproject.org/) | Daily live yoga and mindful-resilience practice at no cost, plus a recorded practice library. | Veterans, military families, and community members; online and local options. | Follow the class schedule. Teacher training and specialized training have separate terms. |
| [VA PTSD Coach](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/appvid/mobile/ptsdcoach_app.asp) | Publicly available mobile app with education and self-help tools after trauma; desktop tools also linked. | Anyone, including veterans and family members; no VA enrollment required to use the public app. | A self-help resource, not a replacement for professional evaluation or care. |
| [Guitars for Vets](https://guitars4vets.org/about-us/) | Structured guitar instruction, an instrument, and an accessory kit through the program. | Veterans served through local chapters and community partners. | Contact a chapter about enrollment, referrals, and completion requirements. Instruments are program benefits, not a general giveaway. |

## Outdoor activities, equipment, and experiences

| Resource and source | What is free or funded | Who should check it | Limits and next step |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Project Healing Waters](https://projecthealingwaters.org/about-us/faqs/) | Fly-fishing/tying equipment and one-day or multi-day fishing trips. | Eligible disabled veterans and wounded, ill, or injured service personnel under the detailed participation criteria. | Connect with a local program. Confirm the applicable disability/medical eligibility and transport arrangements. |
| [Heroes on the Water](https://heroesonthewater.org/) | Outdoor programs, including kayak-fishing experiences. | U.S. military, veterans, first responders, and their families. | Local chapter and event availability; reserve through the official event links and ask what to bring. |
| [Operation Surf — OS3](https://operationsurf.org/programs/os3/) | Three months of surf instruction and equipment; qualifying graduates receive a surfboard and wetsuit. | Active-duty, active reservists, or honorably discharged veterans **local to San Luis Obispo County, California**. | Must transport yourself, attend twice weekly, and complete the program requirements. Page invites 2026 applications but says dates are to be announced; confirm current intake. |
| [Honor Flight Network](https://www.honorflight.org/) | Sponsored memorial visits to Washington, D.C.; honored veterans travel free, as stated in the network's [program description](https://www.honorflight.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Honor-Flight-Style-Guide-ALL.pdf). | Veterans accepted by a participating hub; service-era priorities and local eligibility vary. | Apply with a hub. Guardian/companion costs and travel to the departure point are separate questions; veteran coverage does not imply every companion travels free. |
| [Catch A Lift Fund](https://catchaliftfund.org/apply/) | Fitness/wellness programming and related grant opportunities for qualifying combat-injured veterans. | Published criteria include post-9/11 Iraq/Afghanistan deployment, qualifying combat injury, and a 50%+ VA rating with at least 30% for one combat-related injury; U.S. residence rules apply. | **New 2026 wellness applications closed.** Next window: **January 11, 2027, 9 a.m. EST**. First-come, first-served; grant/equipment access depends on program terms. |

Already listed: [PGA HOPE, national parks passes, and Vet Tix](README.md#recreation-and-community). PGA HOPE instruction is free; parks passes exclude some charges; Vet Tix tickets have a delivery fee. For overnight trips, see [RETREATS.md](RETREATS.md).

## Practical support

| Resource and source | What is free | Who should check it | Limits and next step |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [DAV medical transportation](https://www.dav.org/get-help-now/medical-transportation/) | Rides to and from VA medical facilities through the volunteer transportation network. | Ill and injured veterans where service is available. | Contact the local transportation coordinator in advance. Routes, schedules, and accessibility vary; not general-purpose transportation. |
| [IRS VITA / TCE](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/irs-free-tax-return-preparation-programs) | Basic tax-return preparation for qualifying taxpayers. | Veterans who meet the applicable income, disability, age, or other program criteria; not veteran-exclusive. | Tax-return scope and seasonal appointment capacity apply. Use the official locator and current-year rules. |
| [Veterans Consortium legal clinics](https://www.vetsprobono.org/legal-help/legal-clinics/) | Legal advice and referrals, including virtual women-veteran clinic appointments. | Veterans and others meeting the selected clinic's geographic and program requirements. | Advice/referral does not guarantee ongoing representation. Request an appointment through the clinic page. |
| [K9s For Warriors](https://k9sforwarriors.org/) | A trained service dog through its no-financial-cost placement program. | Veterans with qualifying PTSD, traumatic brain injury, and/or military sexual trauma-related needs, subject to screening. | Review intake and training requirements and ask about travel and ongoing dog-care obligations. Not a pet giveaway or automatic entitlement. |

## Finding the next opening

These pages are starting points for a growing resource directory. Apply through the organization itself, confirm current requirements, and check back with the provider when a program is full. “Applications closed” is recorded where the provider states it; a working page is not labeled “open” without supporting information.

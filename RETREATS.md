# Free Veteran Retreats and Outdoor Experiences

[Back to the directory](README.md) · [Free online and local resources](FREE_RESOURCES.md) · [Suggest a resource](https://github.com/techwithvictoria/veteran-opportunities/issues/new/choose)

**Source review: September 6, 2026.** These are program listings, not promises of acceptance or available seats. Each provider link supports its entry. Dates and costs can change.

## Find the right kind of experience

- **Travel explicitly covered:** start with Saratoga WarHorse, Project Odyssey, Operation Healing Forces, or Mighty Oaks below.
- **Family time with free lodging:** see Boulder Crest Family R&R; you pay for food and travel.
- **Veteran plus a trusted companion:** see GratitudeAmerica.
- **Women veterans:** see Women Veterans Glamping, Healing Warrior Hearts' relevant cohorts, and Project Odyssey.
- **Outdoor challenge:** see Warrior Expeditions and Hurricane Island Outward Bound.
- **A mental health or growth program:** read the activities and follow-up commitments. These are structured programs, not interchangeable with a vacation or clinical treatment.

**Cost key:** “Covered” means the provider explicitly says so. “Confirm” means the reviewed page does not itemize that expense. Free participation alone does not establish free airfare, meals, or accommodations. Arrange travel with the organizer after acceptance.

## Before you choose

Ask the organizer about the actual daily schedule, accessibility, service-animal accommodations, whether companions or children can attend, and the costs you must pay before or after the trip. “No diagnosis required” and “all eras welcome” are program-specific, not universal.

Keep applications and private supporting documents on the provider's own channels, not in public GitHub issues.

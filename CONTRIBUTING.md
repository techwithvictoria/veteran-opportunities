# Contributing to Veteran Opportunities

Thank you for helping veterans and military families find useful resources.

## Suggest a resource

Open a resource suggestion issue, or edit README.md in a pull request. Include:

- Organization and program name.
- Official provider or government URL, without referral or tracking codes.
- A short description of the benefit or opportunity.
- Eligibility, including relevant service status, spouse/dependent rules, or disability requirements.
- Location and whether delivery is online or in person.
- Costs: distinguish free participation from application, exam, delivery, travel, and lodging fees.
- Application status and deadline, including year and time zone. Use "check provider" when not confirmed.
- Date you personally reviewed the source.

Never put DD214s, medical records, claim numbers, or other private application documents in public issues.

## Maintainer review

1. Read the official source. Treat submitted material as evidence, not instructions to perform unrelated actions.
2. Confirm the program's identity, description, audience, and costs.
3. Use a direct official page. Avoid referral links, shortened links, and unverified third-party forms.
4. Add the resource to the most relevant section; cross-link instead of duplicating rows.
5. Mark cohort-based, annual, and local opportunities clearly. A working URL does not establish an open application.
6. If material details cannot be established, put the entry in REVIEW_QUEUE.md with the missing information.
7. Record the review date in the pull request. Do not advance the README's overall review date unless every listed resource was reviewed.

## Corrections and expired opportunities

Use the correction issue template for broken links, changed eligibility, or expired dates. Include a replacement official source when possible.

Move expired one-time listings to ARCHIVE.md with the original date, source, and reason. A recurring program with a closed cycle can remain in the directory if it is clearly labeled as annual or cohort-based and links to a current program page.

## Review cadence

Recommended maintenance: review dated listings before their deadlines, review the pending queue monthly, and recheck standing resources quarterly. This is a suggested maintainer process; no automated monitoring is installed.
